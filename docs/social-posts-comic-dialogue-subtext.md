**Post 2 (Quick Tip):**
The Golden Rule of Comic Dialogue: Show, Don't Tell. 🤫
If your character is visibly furious, they don't need to say "I'm angry!" Let the art do the heavy lifting and use dialogue for subtext.
Learn more: [Link] #Webcomics #ComicCreator

**Post 3 (Layout Tip):**
How do you show people interrupting each other in a comic? 🗣️
Use the "Overlapping Speech Bubble" layout! Draw the second bubble cutting off the first to simulate chaos and tension.
