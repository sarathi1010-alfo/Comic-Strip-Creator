# Social Media Distribution: Post-Apocalyptic Comics

## Twitter/X (Quick Tips & Insights)
1. **Tweet 1:** "Want to make your post-apocalyptic comic feel more real? Ditch the bright colors. Use a desaturated palette (browns, grays, sickly greens) and let a single bright color act as a warning or a sign of hope. 🎨☣️ #ComicArt #PostApocalyptic #Webcomics"
2. **Tweet 2:** "In the wasteland, what a character carries is their backstory. A pristine gas mask tells a different story than a duct-taped one. Design gear with purpose! 🎒🗡️ #CharacterDesign #ComicArtist"
3. **Tweet 3:** "Tension is everything in a survival comic. Try the 'Claustrophobic Layout': 9 to 12 tight, dark panels focused on extreme close-ups. Make your readers feel the panic. 😰📖 Read the guide: [Link]"
