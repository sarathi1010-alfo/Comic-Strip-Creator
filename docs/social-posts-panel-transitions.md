# Social Media Posts: Comic Panel Transitions

## Twitter / X
1. **Quick Tip:** Want to slow down the pacing of your comic? Use a "Moment-to-Moment" transition! Show a micro-progression of time (like an eye blinking) across three panels to build unbearable tension. ⏱️ #ComicArt #Pacing #ComicFlow
2. **Theory Time:** The magic of comics doesn't happen in the panels; it happens in the "gutter"—the blank space between them. That's where the reader's imagination stitches the story together! 🧠✨ #Webcomics #ArtTips
3. **Did You Know?** "Action-to-Action" is the most common panel transition in Western comics. It's the workhorse that moves the plot forward efficiently without wasting space. Master it first! 🏃‍♂️💨 #ComicCreation
