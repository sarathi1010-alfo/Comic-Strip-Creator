# Social Media Posts: Comic Storyboarding Guide

## Twitter / X
1. **Quick Tip:** Don't start drawing your final comic pages without a storyboard! Thumbnails save you hours of revisions and ensure your pacing is perfect. Sketch fast, fail fast, and refine. ✏️ #ComicArt #Storyboarding #ComicFlow
2. **Layout Tip:** Struggling to fit speech bubbles into your panels? The storyboard phase is where you fix this. Always plan the negative space for text *before* you ink! 💬 #Webcomics #ArtTips
3. **Did You Know?** The 180-degree rule isn't just for movies. Keeping your characters on the same axis in a comic scene prevents visual confusion and keeps the reader oriented. #ComicHistory #VisualStorytelling
