# Social Media Distribution: Comic Book Villains

## Twitter/X (Quick Tips & Insights)
1. **Tweet 1:** "A villain shouldn't just be 'evil for the sake of evil.' The best antagonists are a dark reflection of the hero. Give them a relatable motivation and watch your story come alive. 🦹‍♂️ #ComicWriting #Storytelling"
2. **Tweet 2:** "Shape language matters! Want your villain to look dangerous subconsciously? Use sharp, jagged, and triangular shapes in their silhouette. Leave the soft circles for the sidekicks. 📐🎨 #CharacterDesign #ComicArt"
3. **Tweet 3:** "The Villain Monologue: don't let it become an exposition dump! Break up their speeches into smaller, visually interesting panels to maintain pacing. Learn how in our latest guide: [Link] #Webcomics #ComicLettering"
