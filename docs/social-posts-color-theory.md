# Social Media Posts: Comic Color Theory

## Twitter / X
1. **Quick Tip:** Want your hero to pop off the page? Use complementary colors! If your background is a cool, moody blue, give your hero a bright orange jacket. It’s a classic trick that directs the eye instantly. 🎨 #ComicArt #ColorTheory #ComicFlow
2. **Color Psychology:** Colors tell a story before the reader even reads the dialogue. A scene bathed in aggressive reds screams danger, while soft pastels evoke peace. What emotion is your current palette projecting? #Webcomics #ArtTips
3. **Did You Know?** The iconic \"pop art\" comic look wasn't an artistic choice—it was a technical limitation! Early printing presses couldn't handle smooth gradients, so they used Ben-Day dots and primary colors. #ComicHistory #PopArt
