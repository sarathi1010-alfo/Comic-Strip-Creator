# Inter-Cluster Semantic Linking Strategy

While clusters should be tightly interlinked internally, cross-cluster linking establishes the relationship between different high-level topics, forming the overarching Knowledge Graph for the domain.

## Cluster Overlaps

### 1. `Use Cases` <-> `Templates`
- **Overlap:** A specific audience (use-case) needs a specific layout (template).
- **Execution:** On `/use-cases/marketing`, inject a programmatic block: "Recommended Templates for Marketers", linking to `/templates/social-media` and `/templates/email-newsletter`.
- **Anchor Text:** Exact match template name.

### 2. `Use Cases` <-> `Blog`
- **Overlap:** Audience needs methodology before using the tool.
- **Execution:** On `/use-cases/teachers`, link to `/blog/grading-rubrics-for-comics`.
- **Anchor Text:** "Read our guide on [Blog Title]".

### 3. `Comparisons (VS)` <-> `Editor`
- **Overlap:** Once a user decides our tool is better than the alternative, they need to use it.
- **Execution:** At the bottom of `/vs/comic-strip-vs-storyboard`, add a massive CTA: "Start building your comic strip now -> links to `/editor`".

## Risk Mitigation
Do not over-link between unrelated clusters (e.g., linking `/use-cases/corporate-training` to `/templates/superhero`). It dilutes the semantic relevance. Keep cross-links contextual and helpful to the user journey.
