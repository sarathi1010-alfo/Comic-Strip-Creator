# Phase 2–4 OKRs

## Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Cluster Expansion
**Objective:** Broaden the semantic footprint to capture all related long-tail intents.
- **KR 1:** Expand programmatic pages from 100 to 300.
- **KR 2:** Establish 3 new clusters (e.g., `/vs/`, `/guides/`, `/features/`).
- **KR 3:** Achieve an average CTR > 3% across top 50 pages.
- **Anti-Goal:** Do not build complex backend features yet. Focus on traffic capture.

## Phase 3 (Months 7-9): Authority Building
**Objective:** Prove to search engines that we are the definitive entity for "Comic Strip Creation".
- **KR 1:** Publish 20 long-form, high-EEAT blog posts supporting the programmatic clusters.
- **KR 2:** Automate internal linking widgets across 100% of the site.
- **KR 3:** Reach 500 total indexed pages.
- **Anti-Goal:** Do not acquire low-quality backlinks. Focus on semantic authority.

## Phase 4 (Months 10-12): Dominance & SGE Prep
**Objective:** Dominate AI Overview (SGE) citations and maximize conversion.
- **KR 1:** Reach 1,000 indexed pages.
- **KR 2:** Implement exact-match FAQ schema on top 20% of traffic-driving pages.
- **KR 3:** Hit 1,000 organic tool exports/month.
- **Anti-Goal:** Do not ignore content decay. Must prune/update Phase 1 content.
