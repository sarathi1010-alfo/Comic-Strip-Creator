## Twitter / X (Quick Tips)

2. "Don't use generic fonts for your comic! A horror comic needs a jagged font, while a comedy strip needs something bouncy. Font choice sets the tone. 🦇🤡 #comicart"
3. "The Z-Path is crucial in Western comics. Always place the first speaker's bubble higher and further left. Guide the reader's eye! 👁️➡️"
